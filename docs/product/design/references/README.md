# References: what Tucker's own boards say

Tucker asked for his Pinterest boards to be used as visual inspiration. This
is the read of them. It sets the ceiling of the quality bar: the reference
studies are the floor, and these pins are what the build is reaching for.

[`references.yaml`](references.yaml) lists the pins by link, each tagged with
the mood it serves and what it teaches. The images are other people's work
and are not stored here.

## How this was read

- Read 2026-11-24. His profile has 19 public boards and about 3,800 pins.
- 1,173 pins were sampled from 15 boards, laid out as 121 labelled contact
  sheets, and read section by section by seven independent readers. The pins
  chosen as bars were then checked directly.
- Boards named here: Design, Keto AI, Branding, Illustration, Prints, Tech,
  Resource, Photography, Aquascaping and Miniatures. A few personal boards
  inform the taste rules and are not named or linked.
- Colour figures come from the dominant colour Pinterest records per pin.
- **Everything below is inference from what he saved.** He has not said why
  he saved any of it. `why_saved` in the YAML is his to fill in.

## The short version

His saved taste is light, restrained and systematic, with one loud accent at
a time. On the Design board sample, 57 percent of pins are light, 19 percent
dark and 7 percent vivid. The most distinctive things he saves are not web
pages. They are diagrams, spec sheets, instrument panels and specimen grids.

## Taste rules

Each of these repeats across several boards.

1. **A neutral base and exactly one loud accent.** White, cream, charcoal or
   black, then one colour.
2. **Systems over singles.** Specimen grids, logo systems shown as a family,
   many variants of one idea in a regular grid.
3. **Instruction as an aesthetic.** Annotated diagrams, spec sheets with
   title blocks and dimension lines, process sheets. He saves how things are
   made, not only the finished thing.
4. **Huge numerals, tiny labels, hairline rules.** A single oversized number
   or ring is often the hero of a screen.
5. **Radial forms.** Ring gauges, rotary knobs, chord diagrams, concentric
   rings, ripples and waves.
6. **Thin, uniform line work.** Monochrome line illustration is his default.
7. **Material and finish.** Foil, emboss, letterpress, kraft, wood, with the
   tool or the hand sometimes in frame.
8. **Dense data made calm.** Tables and dashboards at low contrast.
9. **Scale through emptiness or miniature.** A small figure in a large
   field. Mist, reflection and still water recur in the nature boards.
10. **Two or three colours at most** in print, packaging and identity.

**Conspicuously absent:** serif display type at scale, pastel gradients,
glossy 3D, stock photographs of people, ornament for its own sake, and glow
that blooms across a whole screen.

**Weak signal:** the UI, Web, Presentation and Infographics sections are
largely template work of their era. Weight the exceptions, not the volume.

## Still Water

**Supports.** Pale interfaces with hairline rules and one accent (`K01-02`,
`K02-10`). One oversized numeral as the hero (`K01-17`). A thin mono clock on
white with tiny data rows (`D02-10`). A serif-set invoice (`D17-07`).
Specimen grids in monochrome line (`D07-07`). Wave patterns in a tight teal
family (`K10-11`). A palette lifted from a misty sea, pale cream down to deep
teal (`D20-11`): the Still Water to Deep Water ramp in one image.

**Challenges.** His light pins are more often warm cream or white than cool
pale. The accent he saves most on light grounds is orange or yellow, not
teal. He saves almost no serif display type. The serif in this mood comes
from his own manual, which outranks his saves.

**Direction.** Keep the manual's tokens and serif. Borrow three things from
the boards: one oversized numeral or ring as a page's hero, huge numerals
beside tiny uppercase labels, and specimen grids for collections.

## Deep Water

**Supports.** Blue-black screens with one luminous orb or ring (`K01-26`,
`K06-05`). Navy with gold foil (`K09-01`). A monumental numeral on a navy
spread (`D17-22`). Data as light on a void (`D13-05`, `D13-14`). A glowing
planted tank in a black room (`A03-04`).

**Challenges.** Most of his dark pins are neutral graphite or black, not
blue-black. Their light is often gold or white. There is no monumental serif
in his saves. Monumental numerals do that job.

**Direction.** Make light the content: one luminous ring or orb as the hero.
Treat dark as a material, tonal with one luminous or metallic detail. Let
numerals carry scale as readily as serif titles. Never neon.

## Cyber

**Supports.** This mood has the strongest evidence of the three. The z0lver
section of Branding is 95 percent dark with a single cyan: concentric rings,
corner-cut frames, percentage readouts (`B03-20`, `B03-18`). Cyan gauges on
black (`D09-28`). Boxed mono readouts and node graphs (`D01-04`, `D01-09`).
Dark maps with points of signal colour (`D09-15`, `D09-59`). Real control
consoles and lit hardware (`D08-02`, `T01-18`). An instrument panel with
huge numerals and one orange arc (`D02-02`).

**Challenges.** His dark interfaces use one dominant signal, cyan, with
orange or amber second. Magenta does not appear. Glow is sparing: on a dial,
a gauge, an arc. His gamer saves are interfaces and hardware, not characters
or game art.

**Direction.** Structure over glow: panels, readouts, dials, status lights.
Make amber the second signal in place of the study's magenta. Lean on real
instruments as much as film interfaces. Maps and node graphs are native
imagery. The radar on the home screen is right.

## The constant

Radial forms recur across unrelated boards: ring gauges, rotary knobs, chord
diagrams, concentric rings, ripples (`D13-14`, `D10-02`, `D02-02`, `K06-05`,
`B03-20`). That supports the suggestion in
[`../mood-ideas.md`](../mood-ideas.md) to make a circle the motif every mood
shares.

## Sides of him the boards add

Suggestions for [`../mood-ideas.md`](../mood-ideas.md), not requirements.

- **The instrument maker.** Pale panels, dials, dot-matrix displays, huge
  numerals, one orange accent (`D02-02`, `D02-06`, `D08-04`).
- **The tinkerer.** Exposed boards, labelled parts, pinouts, schematics
  (`T01-03`, `T01-28`).
- **The hand-letterer.** Cream paper, black ink, flourished capitals, the pen
  in frame (`D19-10`, `D17-34`, `D19-27`).
- **The aquascaper.** Miniature landscapes built in glass and lit in a dark
  room. This one feeds both water moods directly.
- **The wayfinder.** Big arrows, numerals and colour-coded routes at
  building scale.
