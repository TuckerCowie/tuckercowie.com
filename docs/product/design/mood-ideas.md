# Mood ideas: suggestions and an open invitation

Tucker asked what moods might be inferred from his experience, strengths and
attributes, then set the answer aside as suggestions: "Include those as
suggestions not requirements and as an invitation to brainstorm more."

Nothing here is a requirement. The first build ships Still Water, Deep Water
and Cyber. This file is where the next moods get argued.

## A frame

**Same water, different vessel.** Tucker's being statement is still water
that runs deep, flowing freely within its banks, and water takes the shape of
what holds it. The content is the water and each mood is a vessel. Still
Water and Deep Water are the self, at the surface and underneath. Every other
mood is a side of him that the same self can take the shape of.

## One constant

A pattern that survives every shift, so a reader who looks closely can find
it. The studies already share one by accident: a circle. Still Water has a
ripple, Deep Water a luminous ring, Swiss a red disc, Cyber a radar sweep,
Vellum a detail marker. It could be made deliberate.

Sides not yet given a mood: the teacher, the maker, the writer.

## Open questions

1. Is the frame right, and should the circle be the constant?
2. Does Cyber stand for the gamer only, with foresight left to Deep Water?
3. Should the facilitator come back as Workshop, rebuilt in the sticky-note
   colours of his own boards? What was wrong with Workbench as rendered?
4. Which of these are not true sides of him, and what is missing?
5. Should Field Guide carry his mission line as its epigraph?
6. Should the switcher name the side ("Swiss, the designer"), with one short
   Moods page that states the thesis in his words?

## A suggested order

Ledger first, because it is cheap and it proves the claim that the content
stands alone. Then Swiss and Vellum, which have reference material. Then
Workshop and Darkroom. Then Liner Notes and Field Guide.
