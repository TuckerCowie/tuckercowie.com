## Frame 02
Frame title: 2 Smart Pool App - Background

Heading: Background

Body: After a 3D printed prototype of a "smart skimmer" IoT device survived in the founder's pool for a year, he decided to establish a team and share his invention with the world.

We set out to revolutionize DIY pool maintenance so tech-savvy pool owners can skim smarter and swim safer.

Goal chips (all bold):
- Make DIY pool maintenance easier
- Enable real-time awareness of pool status at home

Heading: User Insights
