## Frame 26
Frame title: 26 Repositioning - Background

Heading: Background

Body, paragraph 1: After acquiring and retaining 30+ recurring customers for a novel high-tech pool service at home, we discovered our name was getting the way of organic traction. The word "Keto" worked against us instead of carrying the brand forward on its own.
("getting the way of" is as written.)

Goal chips (bold):
- Differentiate the Keto brand before launching flagship product
- Separate the consumer products from the B2B brand
- Leverage seasonal pool industry trend during spring 2021 to rapidly acquire new users
