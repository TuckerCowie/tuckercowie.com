## Frame 37
Frame title: 37 48hr Visiontype - Details

Heading: Details

Body, left: Electricity usage in industrial enterprises can have huge costs implications (as written) if it is not controlled. Closed-loop systems like midstream oil & gas pipelines allow for more accurate predictions and assumptions regarding

Body, right (continues the left column): electricity consumption since it is highly correlated to operational parameters. We set out to cast vision for how an electricity broker might provide consumption advice during real-time pipeline operations.

Goal chips:
- Incorporate energy market prices into operational variables for pipelines
- Expose peak loads to avoid usage penalties proactively
- Reduce operational costs overall

My Role (bullets):
- Helped create and facilitate initial discovery
- Collaborated with VP Product, SME, and VP Sales to make rapid iterations
- Wireframed and Designed final deliverable

Methodology (bullets):
- Customer discovery meeting (morning)
- Whiteboard user flow session in (afternoon) (as written)
- Paper prototyping with Balsamiq (Day 2)
- Rapid visual design with Sketch (Day 3)

Outcome: We delivered a visionary prototype within 48 hours of initial discovery. The "visiontype" was used in various strategy sessions as an artifact to find funding and partnerships. Discussions about implementation opportunities remain on the table to this day.
