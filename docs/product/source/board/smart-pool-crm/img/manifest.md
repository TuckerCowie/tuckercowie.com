## Notes on size and legibility

Captions marked as a heading only (component-exploration, solution, portrait) have no sentence of their own on the board, so the frame heading is used.

Stay legible when small (thumbnail to about 400px wide): portrait.jpg, solution.jpg (as a pattern of screens, not for reading), component-exploration.jpg (the cards read as a pattern), work-environment.jpg (desk photo reads, the Miro screenshot does not), service-blueprint.jpg (lane names and the large notes read, small notes do not).

Need full width (at least 900px) to be read: competitive-analysis.jpg, product-lifecycle.jpg and user-flow.jpg (the diagram part is only decorative at small size), story-map.jpg (a 6:1 strip, a texture when small), partner-interview.jpg (text is only readable at its native 662px).

Source limits: partner-interview.jpg (long side 790px), portrait.jpg (753px) and story-map.jpg (295px high) are below or near the 900px goal because the board screenshots do not hold more pixels. Do not enlarge them past native size.
