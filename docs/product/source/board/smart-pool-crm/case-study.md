## Frame 12
Frame title: 12 Smart Pool CRM - Details

Heading: Details

Body: The $15B pool services industry is extremely fragmented. This means no company operates the same way and only a few operational standards have emerged. After successfully creating an at-home monitoring solution using the smart skimmer, we moved to test a separate product hypothesis regarding pool service companies: How might the smart skimmer disrupt the way pool *professionals* operate?
(The word "professionals" is set in italics.)

Goal chips:
- Make professional pool service more efficient
- Proactively solve pool issues for customers remotely
- Enable new business models for service companies struggling to scale

My Role (bullets):

- Provide creative direction for team
- Establish team operations and processes
- Fully own the UX lifecycle

Methodology (numbered):

## Frame 18
Frame title: 18 Smart Pool CRM - Customer Insights

Heading: Partner Insights

Quote: "For somebody who just calls in and says: 'our motor is out,' we would just automatically schedule them [for service]..."

Body: Through interviews with partners in our pilot program, we discovered service companies would prefer *not* to do routine cleanings and instead make most money on one-time maintenance work orders.
("not" is set in bold italics.)

INSIGHT: Pros need better ways to expose customer pool issues sooner while decreasing time spent on the phone, dispatching technicians in order to increase revenue and reduce costs
