both and where each falls short of the saved reference pins. "Ours" and "the
reference" below replace A and B after the fact.

### Round 1

#### Still Water, Home: ours picked (clear)

ours reads as one confident typographic statement and answers "who and what" right away. The name is set at about 115px in a light bookish serif across 48 to 674px. Below it comes a dark serif role line, "Product and design leader." (about 28px). Under that, two current roles sit in small grey sans. The motto drops into grey italic at 7.1:1 contrast, so it supports the role instead of competing with it. The vertical rhythm is even: about 16 to 19px inside each group and about 35 to 40px between groups (name 221, role 277, meta 320, quote 379, body 441, rules 566, email 717, footer rule 778). The page ends inside the 900px view, so the home really is one screen. The three destinations get real size: about 42px serif headings under grey-teal hairlines (3.3:1), with equal 261px columns and 48px gutters. "Email Tucker" is the only teal text, and the teal dot in the ripple is the only other accent, so the one-accent rule holds. On the phone ours keeps its order. The name fills the 350px measure by y=252, the role and roles follow, the motto breaks at the sense pause ("deep. / Unhurried..."), and the first destination, "01 Work", is visible above the fold with 20px gutters on both sides. the reference has a document-like left rail and a numbered nav, but the first read fails. The nav is seven items with a broken sequence: "02 WORK", an unnumbered "TUCKER COWIE", "04 WORKS" and "07 SPECIMEN". The motto, about 30px italic in full ink, outranks the role line, about 20px grey sans. The name, about 66px, is outweighed by the 387px ripple. The hero stack is cramped (25 to 29px gaps) while the lower half is slack (53 to 84px). There is a widowed "talks.". The fold slices the next band's "Work" heading in half. On the phone, three lines of nav and a 140px decorative circle push the name to y=407, and no destination is visible at all.

Defects of ours, most damaging first:

1. Right column, ripple graphic (x 988-1380, y 229-620): decoration standing in for content, and it floats off the grid. Its top (229) sits 8px below the name baseline (221), and its bottom (620) lands in the middle of the destination headings (608-648). That leaves 158px of dead band above it and 158px below. The teal dot pulls the eye second, ahead of the role line. Either lock its top to the HOME label (108) and its bottom to the destination rule (566), or replace it with a real work artifact at readable size.
2. Header right (x 1191-1382): the mood switcher is a bordered chip labelled "STILL WATER" next to two unlabelled glyph buttons (a circle and a square). It is the only boxed control in the chrome, it reads as app UI rather than a document, and the icons mean nothing to a stranger. On the phone it takes a whole third header row (y 98-125), so the header grows to 142px, 17% of the screen. Make it a plain mono text control, or move it to the footer on mobile.
3. Name stated twice within 200px: a 22px serif wordmark "Tucker Cowie" in the header and the 115px H1 directly below, with a "HOME" label in between that tells the visitor nothing on the home page. On home, drop the HOME label, and either suppress the header wordmark or reduce it to the ripple mark alone.
4. Meta line (y 320-342): "Head of Product and Operations, NarrowStack." and "Independent work as Cowie Creative." share one line, separated only by a gap of about 28px with no divider. It reads as a run-on, and the DRAFT chip then floats at x=677. Stack the two roles as on mobile, or set them as a mono-labelled data row with a hairline or middot separator.
5. Destination blocks (y 566-677): the mono numeral "01/02/03" (588-598) sits 22px under the rule but only 10px above the 42px heading's cap height, so it reads as attached to the rule. The 42px headings also outrank the 28px role line. Even out the numeral spacing (about 16px above and below), and bring the headings to about 32 to 34px so the role stays the second voice.
6. Quote (y 379-406, x 51): the opening quotation mark is not hung, so "Still" sits about 9px inside the column edge that every other line holds. Hang the punctuation into the margin.
7. Phone body statement (y 500-615): 20px sans breaks with "problems." alone on the fourth line. Tighten the size to about 18px or balance the lines.
8. Ragged right edges with no shared measure on desktop: name 674, meta 737, quote 654, body 616, secondary 611, rules 928. Set one text measure and let only the destination rules run wider.

Where ours falls short of the pins:

1. Information versus ornament: D07-07's labelled specimens, A04-08's annotated stones and D13-13's annotated chart all make the visual carry information. A's right half is an unlabelled target symbol, decoration where a real work artifact or an annotated figure should sit.
2. Grid discipline: D17-06 and D17-07 lock every element to a few edges and margin marks. A's ripple floats with top and bottom (229/620) aligned to nothing, and its text right edges are ragged at 674/737/654/616 with no single measure.
3. Small-type data layer: D02-10 puts tiny, exact mono data rows under its giant thin figure. A's mono layer is only HOME, 01/02/03 and a footer, and the roles and context are a loose sentence pair, not a precisely ruled data row.
4. Tonal depth: D20-11 gives a cream-to-deep-teal ramp. A is one flat cool-grey ground with teal only on a dot and a link, so the mood word "deep" has no tonal expression.
5. Chrome restraint: K02-10 makes dense UI calm with a single action. A's header adds a bordered chip and two unlabelled glyph buttons that read as app controls on what should be a quiet document.
