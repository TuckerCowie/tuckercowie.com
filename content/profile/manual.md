## The one-line version

I do my best work as a specialist generalist. Senior judgment applied to complex, multi-disciplinary problems.

Fifteen years across design, product, and development, in one person.

Judgment and craft are the deliverable, not raw throughput.

## What I bring that is rare

*Four domains most teams split across four people.*

1. Move fluently across engineering, product, design craft, and team leadership.
2. Diagnostic problem solver. Fast, accurate pattern recognition from limited information.
3. 3D and spatial thinker. I see systems as structures and hold the whole shape in my head.
4. Precise communicator. Masterful vocabulary, especially in writing.

## My five signature strengths

*Depth, foresight, and the people around me.*

- **Relator** I go deep with a small band of trusted people and do my best work alongside them.
- **Learner** I master a tool or domain before deploying it, and that ramp is investment, not delay.
- **Analytical** I look for the real cause and the evidence underneath a decision.
- **Developer** I get genuine satisfaction from growing the people next to me.
- **Futuristic** I naturally see what should happen next and why, and I can make it vivid.

## How I am wired to think

*Idea-driven, and biased toward finished things.*

- **Idea generator** With a strong bias toward tangible, finished output.
- **Sequential thinker** I break complex processes into clear, logical steps.
- **Future-focused** I set long-range goals and persevere toward them.
- **Blended worker** At home in deep solo work, and assertive leading a group.

## My three motivational threads

*The same three motives, across everything I do.*

1. Master a tool, then make a beautiful, durable thing with it.
2. Improve a working system so it serves its purpose better than before.
3. Develop the person next to me by sharing what I have mastered.

## How I communicate

*Written-first. I let the work speak.*

- Written-first. I do my clearest thinking and communicating in documents.
- Through artifacts. I show through what I build more than through improvised talk.
- Structured and precise, once I have organized my thinking.
- Async by default. I don't need constant real-time contact to stay aligned.

## How I make decisions

*Deliberate, evidence-led, then committed.*

1. **Assemble the facts** Deliberate and evidence-led. I gather, then commit.
2. **Commit with conviction** Strongest on high-stakes calls, where getting it right beats getting it fast.
3. **Refine in motion** A clear deadline and decision owner turn deliberation into momentum.

## Where I do my best work

*Freedom within a clear frame.*

The operating envelope where I reliably produce craft-level work.

- Two to three significant priorities at a time, run at real depth.
- Protected deep-work blocks for building and producing.
- A small, stable team of trusted people I know by name.
- Clear scope and outcome targets, with autonomy over how I get there.
- Predictable rhythm with intensity variation, not a constant sprint.

## How to get my best work

*Give me a frame, then room to run.*

- Give me concrete problems and outcome targets, then trust my process.
- Hold a weekly, written-agenda one-on-one.
- Let me master the tool before you judge the output.
- Defend my scope so I can go deep instead of wide.
- Pair me with an action-biased partner who pushes things to ship.

## What I care about in the work

*What keeps me engaged.*

- **Finished work** Tangible, something I can put my name on and point to later.
- **Growth** Evidence that someone grew because of my attention.
- **Craft** Beauty in the output, not just function.
- **Good systems** As a form of stewardship.
- **Real outcomes** Work that connects clearly to something that matters.

## What you can count on

*Steady, and true to my word.*

- High-quality, durable output I stand behind.
- A calm, stable presence in complex or high-pressure moments.
- Deep investment in the few people I work closely with.
- Commitments kept and standards held.
- Honest, direct thinking, offered constructively.

## In one line

Aim deep judgment and craft at hard, multi-disciplinary problems, give me a clear frame and room to run, and I'll build things that last.
