Electricity usage in industrial enterprises can have huge cost implications if it is not controlled. Closed-loop systems like midstream oil & gas pipelines allow for more accurate predictions and assumptions regarding electricity consumption since it is highly correlated to operational parameters.

We set out to cast vision for how an electricity broker might provide consumption advice during real-time pipeline operations.

## Step 1

Analysts “plan” for various operating conditions by creating playbooks in advance of scheduled shifts.

## Step 2

Operators monitor variables and conditionally switch “plays” to optimize for cost and production.

## Step 3

The whole ops team evaluates performance and creates better plans for the next production shift.
