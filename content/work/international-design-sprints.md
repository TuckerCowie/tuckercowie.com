Arundo hired various SMEs and Data Scientists with experience from deep within “asset-heavy” industrial enterprises. Much of their role included project managing 90-day paid proof-of-concept engagements with super-majors (world's largest oil companies).

I trained our team of designers to facilitate design sprints and educate non-design colleagues about how to leverage design thinking early on in project discovery.
